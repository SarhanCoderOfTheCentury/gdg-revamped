About
GDG@DSU

GDG@DSU started its journey with the aim to make a community of tech enthusiasts eager to learn outside the classroom. Through workshops, events, and hands-on projects, we provide opportunities for students to expand their skills, connect with like-minded peers, and prepare for a successful career in the industry.
